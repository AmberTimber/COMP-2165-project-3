# Asset sources

The 31 icon PNGs in `icons/` are 48 × 48 tiles extracted from the `cookie_clicker_icons.png` image supplied by the project owner. The complete image is kept at `Assets/Clicker/SpriteSheets/cookie_clicker_icons.png`, with tile choices in `IconMap.csv`. Ownership and redistribution permissions for this supplied image have not been verified.

Interface sound effects by Kenney, from https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds, licensed CC0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/. Unmodified WAV files.
